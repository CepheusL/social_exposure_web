About this site

This site hosts data explorers for research projects that measure what firms say on earnings conference calls. The measures cover thousands of listed firms worldwide and reach back to 2001. Each explorer shows trends, rankings and firm pages, and offers the full data as CSV downloads.

More projects will be added as they are released.
