Methodology

The measures come from the transcripts of quarterly earnings calls held by listed firms. A machine learning keyword discovery algorithm (King, Lam and Roberts, 2017) starts from a short list of initial climate change bigrams and learns the other bigrams that call participants use when they discuss climate change. Seeded with topic bigrams, the same search yields three sets for opportunities, regulatory shocks and physical shocks. The three sets do not overlap and each is part of the overall set.

Exposure is computed per call as (climate change bigrams in the call / all bigrams in the call) × 1,000. Annual values average the firm's calls in the year.

Metrics

MetricDefinition (per 1,000 bigrams in the call)
ExposureClimate change bigrams
RiskClimate change bigrams in sentences with the words risk or uncertainty or their synonyms
Positive sentimentClimate change bigrams weighted by the positive tone words (Loughran and McDonald, 2011) in the same sentence
Negative sentimentClimate change bigrams weighted by the negative tone words in the same sentence. The website shows it as a positive count
